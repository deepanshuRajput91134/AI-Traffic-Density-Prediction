Problem Statement

The Urban Traffic Congestion Challenge

Why legacy traffic management systems are failing modern smart cities

🚗

Severe Congestion

Rapid vehicular growth in Tier-1 and Tier-2 Indian cities creates severe bottlenecks at major traffic intersections during peak commute windows.

⏱️

Economic & Time Loss

Commuters waste over 100+ hours stuck in traffic annually, resulting in millions of liters of wasted fuel and heightened atmospheric emissions.

🚦

Static Control Systems

Current signal timers operate on static schedules that cannot react to weather disruptions, accidents, or sudden vehicle surges in real time.

💡 Core Need: An adaptive AI system that simultaneously predicts density and computes optimal routes. Proactive > Reactive

Graph Routing

Dijkstra Route Optimization Algorithm

Traffic-aware shortest path calculation on simulated smart city network

City Graph Topology

Model consists of 6 interconnected key smart city hubs:

🏢 1. City Center 💼 2. Tech Park 🏡 3. North Junction 🛣️ 4. South Interchange 🏬 5. East Corridor ✈️ 6. Airport Hub

Connected by 9 bidirectional road segments with standard base transit weights.

Congestion Penalty Formula

Effective Edge Weight: W_effective = W_base × Penalty Factor

🟢 Low Traffic Density: 1.0× (Normal travel time)
🟡 Medium Traffic Density: 1.4× (+40% delay penalty)
🔴 High Traffic Density: 2.0× (+100% heavy jam penalty)

Reroutes commuter flow around bottlenecks to achieve global city-wide balance.

✓ Outputs primary optimal path + alternative backup route Implemented in backend/app/services/routing_service.py

Computer Vision

OpenCV Vehicle Detection Pipeline

Bridging physical CCTV infrastructure with intelligent predictive analytics

5-Stage Vision Workflow

  1. Frame Ingestion: Ingest camera stream or recorded video frame.
  2. Preprocessing: Grayscale conversion & Gaussian filter smoothing.
  3. Background Subtraction: MOG2/KNN to isolate foreground motion.
  4. Contour Analysis: Bounding box extraction with area thresholds.
  5. Automated Count: Tracks vehicles crossing virtual detection tripwire.

Calculated count feeds directly into ML density prediction endpoint.
